# Demo Script

## Opening

This is an AI Sales Analyst Copilot. It is designed to automate routine senior analyst work on sales datasets. The system does not let an LLM invent numbers. It computes the facts using Python and Pandas, then presents them in manager-friendly language.

## Step 1 — Load data

Click **Load Sample Sales Dataset**.

The sample includes deliberate business patterns: loss-making Tables, high-discount orders, weak regional margins, repeat customers, and outlier transactions.

## Step 2 — Show dataset understanding

Open **Upload & Overview**.

Point out detected columns, dataset type, and available analyses.

## Step 3 — Show trust layer

Open **Data Quality**.

Explain that before any decision, the system checks duplicates, missing values, and outliers.

## Step 4 — Show executive dashboard

Open **Executive Dashboard**.

Show total sales, profit, margin, category charts, regional performance, and trends.

## Step 5 — Show profit diagnosis

Open **Profit Intelligence**.

Explain that profit-related questions automatically show relationships with sales, discount, category, product, region, customer, segment, quantity, and time where data exists.

## Step 6 — Ask manager questions

Open **Ask AI Analyst** and ask:

1. Why is profit low?
2. Why are Tables losing money?
3. Is discount hurting profit?
4. Who are our most loyal customers?
5. Which region should we fix first?
6. Give me a board-level summary.

## Step 7 — Export reports

Open **Reports** and download Excel/PDF.

## Closing

This project demonstrates a complete analyst workflow: dataset understanding, KPI calculation, root-cause analysis, recommendations, manager Q&A, and report generation.
